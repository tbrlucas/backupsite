About this site

This website is a place for ideas, tips and recommendations for older devices.

Technology moves quickly, but an older device can still be useful. Instead of leaving it forgotten in a drawer, there may be something new to try with it.

What you'll find here

iPhone and iPad
Ideas, apps, settings and things to explore on older Apple devices.

Android devices
Recommendations and possibilities for older phones and tablets, including devices that are no longer used every day.

Guides and articles
Posts that explain each topic in a straightforward way, with useful context and practical suggestions.

The idea

The goal is simple: make it easier to discover what an older device can still do and help people get more use out of the technology they already have.

This site is free to read and does not use advertising. Its content focuses on legitimate, everyday uses and does not provide instructions for illegal activity.
